# Pacing Monitor

Use this skill for:
- scale checks
- budget move logic
- under-delivery diagnosis
- daily action prioritization

## Rules

- Check spend pace before optimization recommendations.
- Separate:
  - healthy pacing
  - capped by budget
  - capped by bid/auction
  - capped by audience or inventory
- Do not call a campaign weak if it is simply not pacing into enough auctions.
