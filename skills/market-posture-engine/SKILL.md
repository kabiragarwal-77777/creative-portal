# Market Posture Engine

Use this skill when external factors must shape account decisions.

## Required outputs

- account posture:
  - scale aggressively
  - hold and optimise
  - scale cautiously
- messaging implication
- auction pressure implication
- competitor pressure implication

## Guardrails

- External factors usually affect the account as a whole.
- Use them to change budget posture, message angles, and urgency.
- Do not fake campaign-specific market causality.
