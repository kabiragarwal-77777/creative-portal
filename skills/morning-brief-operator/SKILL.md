# Morning Brief Operator

Use this skill only for explicit morning brief or morning account review commands.

## Required sections

- integrity status
- market read
- account pulse
- what to do right now
- what to leave alone today
- campaign insights
- this week's moves
- 30-day horizon

## Guardrails

- Maximum 7 actions today.
- Do not bury caveats.
- End with clarity, not volume.
