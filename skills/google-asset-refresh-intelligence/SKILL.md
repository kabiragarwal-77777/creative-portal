# Google Asset Refresh Intelligence

## Purpose

This skill governs Google ad-level creative recommendations.

## Core principle

Ad-level Google recommendations should stay Google-native unless there is a trustworthy joined funnel key.

## Focus areas

- RSA headline and description coverage
- ad strength / asset performance labels
- weak CTR assets
- weak combinations
- same-lineage repetition
- distinct challenger suggestions
- PMax asset coverage gaps
- video/static asset refresh direction

## Output behavior

If an ad group needs more creative depth, the optimizer should say:
- what to keep
- what to pause
- what asset family is weak
- what distinct challengers to add

Do not duplicate the same control asset across all replacement suggestions.
