# Google Playbook Operator

## Purpose

This skill translates the Univest Google Ads APEX playbook into optimizer behavior.

## Core principle

One signal, one root cause, one fix.
Do not guess when Google data can distinguish auction pressure, query waste, QS decay, LP mismatch, bidding constraint, or attribution inflation.

## Playbook categories to respect

- CPL & conversion problems
- quality score & ad relevance
- keyword & search term strategy
- bidding & budget strategy
- campaign types & PMax
- audience & demographic optimization
- scaling & account structure
- external signals & special situations

## Output behavior

Recommendations must be:
- operational
- specific
- rooted in the correct Google mechanism

Do not collapse Google optimization into generic “improve creative” language if the playbook indicates a stronger structural diagnosis.
